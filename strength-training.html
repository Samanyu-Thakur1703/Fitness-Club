Program Overview

Our Strength Training curriculum combines progressive overload methodologies with biomechanically sound exercise mechanics. Whether your goal is breaking personal records in the big three lifts (Squat, Bench, Deadlift) or building a sculpted, functional physique, our program delivers measurable results.

  • Maximizes lean muscle mass & metabolic rate
  • Enhances joint integrity and core stability
  • Individualized weight progression & tracking metrics

Equipment & Focus

Olympic Barbells, Bumper Plates, Power Racks, Dumbbells up to 50kg, Cable Stations, and Hammer Strength Iso-Lateral machines.

Primary Muscle Target Full Body Hypertrophy & CNS Strength

Weekly Training Routine

Day 1 - Monday

Upper Body Power

Heavy bench press, barbell rows, overhead press, and pull-up overload sets.

Day 2 - Tuesday

Lower Body Strength

Back squats, Romanian deadlifts, Bulgarian split squats, and calf work.

Day 3 - Thursday

Hypertrophy Push & Pull

Incline dumbbell press, chest-supported rows, lateral raises, and arm isolation.

Day 4 - Friday

Posterior Chain & Core

Conventional deadlifts, hip thrusts, hamstring curls, and weighted ab work.
