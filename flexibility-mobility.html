Program Overview

Modern lifestyle and intense lifting routines often cause muscular stiffness, tight hip flexors, and rounded shoulders. Our Flexibility & Mobility sessions combine Functional Range Conditioning (FRC), gentle active flows, and breathing methodologies to restore natural posture and athletic fluidity.

  • Relieves lower back, neck, and shoulder tightness
  • Expands active range of motion for deeper squats and lifts
  • Decreases injury risk through tendon and ligament resilience

Tools & Modalities

Theragun Percussive Massagers, Foam Rollers, Mobility Bands, Yoga Blocks, Inversion Tables, and Infrared Heating Mats.
